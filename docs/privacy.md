# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

## About YouTube Shorts Uploader

YouTube Shorts Uploader is a privately operated application used by its owner and authorized administrators to publish and manage authorized content on YouTube channels. It is not offered as a public software-as-a-service product.

## Information accessed

When an authorized administrator connects a Google account or YouTube channel, YouTube Shorts Uploader may access information made available through YouTube API Services that is necessary to:

- authenticate the administrator and confirm authorization;
- identify the connected Google account or YouTube channel;
- determine the permissions granted to the application; and
- upload, publish, or manage content requested by the authorized administrator.

This may include YouTube channel and video identifiers and names, granted permission information, and information about content handled through the application. The exact information available is controlled by Google and YouTube and by the permissions granted to YouTube Shorts Uploader.

## Access tokens and credentials

Access tokens and other credentials are used only to make authorized requests to YouTube API Services. They are treated as sensitive and are not intentionally made public. Administrators should not send tokens, passwords, or other credentials through public channels.

## Use and disclosure

Information accessed through YouTube API Services is used to authenticate and authorize access, identify connected accounts, and perform the requested content-publishing or management actions. Personal information is not sold. YouTube Shorts Uploader does not use Google or YouTube account information for advertising or unrelated tracking.

Google processes information under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). YouTube Shorts Uploader does not control Google's or YouTube's processing of information.

## Data choices

An administrator can revoke YouTube Shorts Uploader's access through the [Google Account permissions settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Requests to delete data associated with YouTube Shorts Uploader can be made by following the [Data Deletion Instructions](../data-deletion/).

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's operation or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
